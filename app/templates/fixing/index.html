✂️ Un-spread

In a document whose sheets are mostly landscape, each landscape sheet is split into its left and right halves; portrait sheets — a single cover page, say — pass through untouched. A portrait document with a few landscape sheets holds wide tables, not spreads, and is skipped whole.
